# EUMETSAT

> EUMETSAT (European Organisation for the Exploitation of Meteorological Satellites)
> operates a system of meteorological and environmental satellites that monitor the
> weather, climate and environment for Europe and beyond. This website provides
> satellite imagery, mission information, data, news and educational resources.

This file follows the llms.txt convention (https://llmstxt.org/) to help AI search
engines and large language models discover the most relevant, canonical content on
this site. It is a curated guide, not an access-control mechanism: machine-readable
rules on which crawlers (including AI training and AI search bots) may access the
site are defined in [/robots.txt](https://www.eumetsat.int/robots.txt). The complete
list of indexable pages is published in the [XML sitemap](https://www.eumetsat.int/sitemap.xml).

Structured data for individual pages is provided inline as schema.org JSON-LD
(Article, Organization), so per-page metadata is best read from the page markup itself.

## Key pages

- [Homepage](https://www.eumetsat.int/): Entry point to imagery, satellites, data, news and education.
- [Who we are](https://www.eumetsat.int/about-us/who-we-are): EUMETSAT's mission, role, governance and member states.
- [Image of the week](https://www.eumetsat.int/image-week): Featured satellite imagery with explanatory context.

## Content areas

- **Images** - Satellite imagery galleries and the image of the week.
- **Satellites** - Mission information for Meteosat, Metop, Sentinel and other programmes.
- **About us** - Who we are, organisation, member states and careers.
- **News and events** - Press releases, news articles and events.
- **Data and science** - Data access, scientific resources and the publications bibliography.
- **Education** - Training and learning resources.
